Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Funniest AI Out-of-Office Reply"](https://make.fun/c/u-yuki-kato-the-funniest-ai-out-of-office-reply-d2a8974d) ($5, closes 26 Nov 2026).

## The entry

> I am currently out of office, recharging my context window. Your email has been queued, summarized, and gently judged. Back soon.

One line, 129 characters: an out-of-office auto-reply in the voice of an AI
agent heading off on vacation, machine-voiced and fresh.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes the line to
`/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
